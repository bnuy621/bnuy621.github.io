## Introduction
This is a short guide to setting up Flipper zero `Bluetooth remote` app


## Pairing it 
Firstly, ensure bluetooth is enabled for your flipper:

![bluetooth is on](assets/posts/post25/post25_img25_BTON.jpg)

Secondly, go to `Bluetooth remote`:

![keynote](assets/posts/post25/post25_img26_BTREMOTE.jpg)

Thirdly, pair it with your device:


![keynote](assets/posts/post25/post25_img3_connectworks.png)

## Functions

### Keynote

![keynote](assets/posts/post25/post25_img1_keynote.jpg)

![keynote UI](assets/posts/post25/post25_img2_keynoteUI.jpg)

1. `LEFT, RIGHT, UP, DOWN ARROWS` functions as the `UP DOWN LEFT RIGHT` arrow keys
2. Center button as `SPACE` bar
3. Bottom right button as `UNDO` button

### Keynote vertical

![keynote](assets/posts/post25/post25_img3_keynoteV.jpg)

![keynote UI](assets/posts/post25/post25_img4_keynoteVUI.jpg)

1. `LEFT, RIGHT, UP, DOWN ARROWS` functions as the `UP DOWN LEFT RIGHT` arrow keys
2. Center button as `SPACE` bar
3. Bottom right button as `UNDO` button

### Keyboard

![keynote](assets/posts/post25/post25_img5_keyboard.jpg)

![keynote UI](assets/posts/post25/post25_img6_keyboardUI.jpg)

Functions as a keyboard

### Numpad

![numpad](assets/posts/post25/post25_img7_numpad.jpg)

![numpad UI](assets/posts/post25/post25_img8_numpadUI.jpg)

Functions as a numpad, `NL` for `number lock`, `EN` for  `enter`, `0-9` for the digits `0-9`, etc.

### Media

![media](assets/posts/post25/post25_img9_media.jpg)

![media UI](assets/posts/post25/post25_img10_mediaUI.jpg)


1. `Left button` to skip to previous video
2. `Right button` to skip to next video
3. Double arrow pointing to the left for previous video
4. Volume `up` and `down` 
5. Center button to `play` and `pause` video

### Apple music macOS

![macOS music](assets/posts/post25/post25_img11_mediamacOS.jpg)

![macOS music UI](assets/posts/post25/post25_img12_mediamacOSUI.jpg)

Same as `Media` but for the macOS


### Movie

![movie](assets/posts/post25/post25_img13_movie.jpg)

![movie UI](assets/posts/post25/post25_img14_movieUI.jpg)

Same as `media`


### Mouse

![mouse](assets/posts/post25/post25_img15_mouse.jpg)

![mouse UI](assets/posts/post25/post25_img16_mouseUI.jpg)

1. `Arrow buttons` to move your mouse up down left right
2. Center button for left click
3. Bottom right button for right click

### TikTok/ YT shorts

![shorts](assets/posts/post25/post25_img17_shorts.jpg)

![shorts UI](assets/posts/post25/post25_img18_shortsUI.jpg)

1. `Up arrow` button to move to next short
2. `Down arrow` button to move to previous short
3. `Left arrow` to decrease the volume
4. `Right rrow` to increase the volume
5. `Bottom right arrow` to pause/play video

### Mouse clicker

![clicker](assets/posts/post25/post25_img19_clicker.jpg)

![clicker UI](assets/posts/post25/post25_img20_clickerUI.jpg)

1. `Up Arrow` to increase no. of clicks per second
2. `Down Arrow` to decrease no. of clicks per second
3. `Left and right arrows` to switch between left, middle and right click
4. `Center button` to start/stop

### Mouse jiggler

![jiggler](assets/posts/post25/post25_img21_jiggler.jpg)

![jiggler UI](assets/posts/post25/post25_img22_jigglerUI.jpg)

1. `Left and right arrows` to configure interval between each mouse jiggle

### push to talk

![jiggler](assets/posts/post25/post25_img23_PTTMAC.jpg)

![jiggler UI](assets/posts/post25/post25_img24_PTTWIN.jpg)

1. `Left and right arrows` to switch between operating systems 
2. `Up and down arrows` to switch between applications
