## Introduction
This is a short guide to setting up Flipper zero `Bluetooth trigger` app

## Pairing it
Firstly, ensure bluetooth is enabled for your flipper:

![bluetooth is on](assets/posts/post25/post25_img25_BTON.jpg)

Secondly, go to `BT trigger`:

![bluetooth is on](assets/posts/post26/post26_img1_BTTRIGGER.jpg)


Thirdly, pair it with your device, in this case i will use my phone:

![bluetooth is on](assets/posts/post26/post26_img2_BTPAIR.png)

## Functions

![bluetooth is on](assets/posts/post26/post26_img3_BTTRIGGERUI.jpg)

1. `UP and DOWN arrows` to configure delay between each shot
2. `RIGHT arrow` to take single shot
3. `LEFT arrow` to reset shot count
