### Q4
Do any imports hint at what this malware does? If so, which imports are they?

`Lab01-01.dll`:

![lab01-01.dll imports](assets/posts/MA/lab1/MA-lab1-1_img1.png)



Ans: It imports `CreateProcessA` from `Kernel32.dll`. This suggests the creation of a process. <https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/processthreadsapi/nf-processthreadsapi-createprocessa> 
It also imports `sleep` from `Kernel32.dll` which suggests the act of pausing a running process. <https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/synchapi/nf-synchapi-sleep>

Lastly, the importing of `WS2_32.dll` suggests network related activities.

`Lab01-01.exe`:

![lab01-01.exe imports](assets/posts/MA/lab1/MA-lab1-1_img8_exeIMPORTS.png)

Ans: It imports `FindFirstFileA`, `FindNextFileA` which hints that it has a function related to searching of files.
