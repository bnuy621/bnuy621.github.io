## Introduction
This post serves to aid in setting up ATAK


## What is ATAK?
ATAK also known as CivTAK is an Android smartphone geospatial infrastructure and situational awareness app. It allows for precision targeting, surrounding land formation intelligence, situational awareness, navigation, and data sharing. More information on it on their website: <https://www.civtak.org/atak-about/>

## Permissions

![EULA agreement](assets/posts/post30/post30_img2_EULA.png)


Read through the permissions:

Location > select `while using this app`:

![location permissions](assets/posts/post30/post30_img4_location.png)

Audio > i selected `don't allow`:

![audio permissions](assets/posts/post30/post30_img5_audio.png)

Locate nearby devices > `allow`:

![locate nearby devices permissions](assets/posts/post30/post30_img6_locationnearby.png)


Notifications > `allow`:

![devices](assets/posts/post30/post30_img7_notifs.png)



Phone calls > `don't allow`:

![phone calls permissions](assets/posts/post30/post30_img8_phonecalls.png)


Music, Audio > `don't allow`:

![music and audio access permissions](assets/posts/post30/post30_img9_audio.png)

![file access](assets/posts/post30/post30_img10_fileaccess.png)

![allow file access pop up](assets/posts/post30/post30_img11_file2.png)

![allow all file access](assets/posts/post30/post30_img12_allowagain.png)
