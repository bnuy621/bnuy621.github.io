## Introduction
This guide covers how to install a custom firmware on the flipper zero. This is useful as most of my guides i make about the flipper zero is done using the momentum firmware.

## Prerequisites
Have qflipper installed.

## Why
Custom firmwares such as Momentum,Unleashed and Roguemaster provide additional and improved features the original firmware does not. For example, if you want an additional feature such as Bad BT (Bluetooth), you will have to install a custom firmware such as Momentum as the official firmware does not have it.

## Selecting your custom firmware
I recommend Momentum as it contains features from Unleashed and RogueMaster. 

## Downloading it
As i will be using Momentum, go to its official Github repository and download the .tgz file from releases (<https://github.com/Next-Flip/Momentum-Firmware/releases/tag/mntm-012>):

![screenshot of releases](assets/posts/post9/post9_img1_releases.png)

## Installing it
Open up qflipper and click on 'upload from file':

![screenshot of releases](assets/posts/post9/post9_img2_UI1.png)

Select your downloaded zip file:

![screenshot of releases](assets/posts/post9/post9_img3_selectfile.png)

A prompt will appear on qflipper, click 'install'. Your flipper zero will now show the state of installation.

![screenshot of releases](assets/posts/post9/post9_img5_install.png)


![screenshot of releases](assets/posts/post9/post9_img4_installing.png)



## Verifying it
After a successful installation, qflipper will now show the name of your custom firmware:

![screenshot of releases](assets/posts/post9/post9_img2_UI1.png)
