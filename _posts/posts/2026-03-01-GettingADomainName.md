## Introduction
This guide will show how to get a domain name for free

## Getting a free domain name from Duckdns.org
I will be using Duckdns.org as it is free. Firstly, go their website and sign in. Afterwards, do the recaptcha and enter the name of your subdomain. If you're using Brave, turn off Brave shields so the recaptcha will work.

![screenshot of duckdns](assets/posts/post1/post1_img1_duckweb.png)

Secondly, enter a subdomain name of your choice as shown below:

![screenshot of form to enter subdomain name](assets/posts/post1/post1_img2_bnuy621.png)

Thirdly, you will be greeted with the configuration details of your domain name. A default ipv4 address will be provided. 

![screenshot of domain name details](assets/posts/post1/post1_img3_domains.png)

Afterwards, try pinging it to ensure it works.

![screenshot of ping results](assets/posts/post1/post1_img4_pingresults.png)
