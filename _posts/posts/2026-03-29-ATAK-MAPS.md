## Introduction
This post aims to guide users how to set up maps on ATAK


## Downloading the maps
Go to <https://github.com/joshuafuller/ATAK-Maps/releases> , download the latest release:

![map download](assets/posts/post31/post31_img1_download.png)

![map download](assets/posts/post31/post31_img2_downloadlocation.png)


## Importing the maps
Open up `ATAK`, click on the `hamburger` icon and select `import`:

![map download](assets/posts/post31/post31_img3_import.png)

Select `Local SD`:

![map download](assets/posts/post31/post31_img4_localSD.png)


`Downloads`:

![download folder](assets/posts/post31/post31_img5_download.png)

Select the zip file:

![zip file](assets/posts/post31/post31_img6_zip.png)

I chose `copy`:

![zip file](assets/posts/post31/post31_img7_importcopy.png)

## Using the map

Click on the map icon (3rd one from the left), you will now see the available maps:

![map](assets/posts/post31/post31_img8_map.png)

Select `Google - Hybrid`:

![map of chad](assets/posts/post31/post31_img9_select.png)

## Downloading the map
To use the maps offline, you will have to download them. To do so, click on the small white arrow at the bottom right:

![map of chad](assets/posts/post31/post31_img10_selectarea.png)

Click on `select area` and your method of selecting the area:

![selecting area](assets/posts/post31/post31_img11_selectarea.png)

Now click on `download`:

![click on download](assets/posts/post31/post31_img12_selected.png)

Create a new tileset:

![select create a new tileset](assets/posts/post31/post31_img13_createnewtileset.png)

Naming it:

![naming your map](assets/posts/post31/post31_img14_namelayer.png)

Now it will be downloading your map:

![downloading map](assets/posts/post31/post31_img15_downloadprogress.png)


## Viewing your map
Click on the toggle switch that says `online`, now it will be `local` which displays the map you just downloaded:

![results](assets/posts/post31/post31_img16_results.png)
