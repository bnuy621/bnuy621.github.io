## Introduction
This guide will show how to get a domain name for free

## Getting one
I will be using Duckdns.org for this as it is free. Firstly, go their website, sign in via one of the following ways, do the recaptcha and enter the name of your subdomain. 
![screenshot of duckdns](assets/posts/post1/post1_img1_duckweb.png)
Secondly, enter a subdomain name of your choice as shown below with me entering 'bnuy621' as the subdomain name.
![screenshot of form to enter subdomain name](assets/posts/post1/post1_img2_bnuy621.png)

Thirdly, you will be greeted with the configuration details of the subdomain name. A default ipv4 address will be provided. 

![screenshot of domain name details](assets/posts/post1/post1_img3_domains.png)

Afterwards, try pinging it to ensure it works.

![screenshot of ping results](assets/posts/post1/post1_img4_pingresults.png)

## Conclusion
This sums up how to get a domain name for free, any other configurations will be covered in an upcoming guide.
